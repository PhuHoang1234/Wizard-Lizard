# 🎵 FREE MUSIC SOURCES FOR WIZARD LIZARD

## ✅ SAFE & LEGAL Options:

### 1. YouTube Audio Library (YouTube's Own Free Music)
- Go to: https://www.youtube.com/audiolibrary
- Search: "fantasy", "medieval", "ambient", "dungeon"
- Download directly from YouTube (these are royalty-free!)
- Perfect for game background music

### 2. Incompetech.com (Kevin MacLeod)
- Website: https://incompetech.com
- Search terms: "Dark", "Ambient", "Fantasy"
- Recommended tracks:
  * "Dark Descent" - dungeon atmosphere
  * "Tenebrous Brothers Carnival" - mysterious
  * "Gregorian Chant" - medieval feel

### 3. Freesound.org (Best for Sound Effects)
- Create free account
- Download high-quality game audio
- Search: "dungeon ambient", "medieval music", "fantasy background"

### 4. Quick Start Files (Use Your Own Music)
- Use ANY music files you own (.mp3, .wav)
- Put them in: Assets/Audio/Music/
- Test the system, replace with game music later

## 🎮 SPECIFIC RECOMMENDATIONS:

### Background Music:
- "YouTube Audio Library" → Search "fantasy ambient"
- Download 2-3 tracks (3-5 minutes each)
- Rename to: background_music_1.mp3, etc.

### Sound Effects:
- Freesound.org → "footstep stone" 
- Freesound.org → "key pickup game"
- Freesound.org → "treasure chest open"
- Freesound.org → "monster growl"

## 🚀 FASTEST SETUP:
1. Go to YouTube Audio Library
2. Search "ambient fantasy"
3. Download 1 background music track
4. Put it in: Assets/Audio/Music/
5. Assign it to AudioManager in Unity
6. Test your game!

## 📝 NOTE:
YouTube Audio Library music is SPECIFICALLY made for content creators and game developers - completely legal to use!
